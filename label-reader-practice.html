The label-reader practice

I turn the package around.

This is for people who read the ingredient list before deciding—and want their attention to become a personal record they can return to, not another one-time lookup.

I read what is actually printed. I keep my own record. I remember what I read.

No universal score. No borrowed verdict. A personal Label Library built from real packages.

Begin with three real labels across two days

1 · ChoosePick three packages already at home whose ingredient lists you would genuinely read.
2 · RecordSave the visible label text, verify it against the package, and notice only the terms you choose.
3 · ReturnCome back on another day and revisit what you read instead of starting from zero.
