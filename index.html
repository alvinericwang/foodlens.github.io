Questions

Does Ingredio need a barcode?

No. You can photograph a visible ingredient list directly. If a barcode is available, Ingredio may use public product data as additional context.

What does a watchlist match mean?

It means recognized text from the visible label matched a term in the watchlist you selected. It is not a determination that a product is safe, unsafe, healthy, or suitable.

Can I rely on the result for an allergy?

No. Ingredio cannot detect undeclared ingredients, manufacturing cross-contact, or every OCR error. Always use the complete current package label and manufacturer information, and seek qualified advice where needed.

What happens to a label scan?

OCR begins on device. Recognized text is sent for processing, and a compressed image may be sent only when OCR confidence is very low. Read the plain-language privacy summary or the full Privacy Policy.
