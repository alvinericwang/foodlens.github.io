Imported food label guide

Start with the printed label—even when the package is unfamiliar.

Imported foods, specialty products, and small brands may be missing from barcode databases. A clear photo of the ingredient panel can help you structure the visible text and review unfamiliar terms.

A verification-first workflow

  1. Find the complete printed ingredient panel. Use the current package rather than relying on front-of-package claims or an older database entry.
  2. Capture the original text clearly. Reduce glare and curvature, keep the camera parallel, and include the beginning and end of the list.
  3. Review the recognized text. Check spelling, punctuation, and line breaks against the package before using a translation or reference.
  4. Treat translation as a reading aid. Keep the original text visible because ingredient names and regional terminology do not always translate one-to-one.
  5. Verify before making a decision. Use the complete current label and manufacturer information whenever accuracy matters.

Where an ingredient scanner can help

Ingredio can start from a photo instead of requiring a barcode. It structures recognized ingredient text, may return ingredient names in your display language, provides plain-language references, and shows visible-text matches from the personal Watchlist you choose.

Recognition varies by label. Language, script, tiny type, damaged packaging, mixed-language panels, and photo quality can all affect OCR and translation. A translated name is not a substitute for the original package text.

What a Watchlist match does not mean

A match means visible label text corresponds to a term you selected. It does not certify that an imported product is safe, authentic, compliant with local rules, free from cross-contact, or suitable for a particular person.

Why barcode data may differ from the package

Product databases can be incomplete or outdated, and the same brand may sell different formulations in different markets. Treat barcode data as context and the complete current package label as the source to verify.
